# Features

## Automatic Tracking
### Your Reps Counted
Pushanova counts your push-ups automatically while you work out. No need to keep count yourself—just start exercising and focus on your form and pace. Choose your preferred tracking method on iPhone or use Apple Watch to count push-ups from your wrist.

## Two Ways to Train
### Progressive Program & Freestyle
Follow a structured program toward 100 push-ups, or train freely with your own sets and repetitions. Start wherever you are and progress at your own pace.

## Apple Watch + iPhone
### One Workout, Two Screens
Start your workout on iPhone and train with Apple Watch while your iPhone keeps your workout information in view. Repetitions, pace, and other workout data stay synchronized so you can use each device for what it does best.

## Workout Metrics
### Reps, Sets, Pace, Heart Rate, Calories
See the details of every workout as you exercise. Pushanova tracks your repetitions, sets, pace, and workout time, with heart rate and calorie data available when supported.

## Progress & Analytics
### See How You're Improving

Every workout becomes part of your history. See your push-ups, sets, pace, heart rate, calories, and other workout data over time with detailed summaries, charts, and calendar views.


## Apple Health
### Your Workout, Part of Your Health Data

Save your Pushanova workouts to Apple Health along with supported workout metrics such as heart rate and calories. Your push-up workouts become part of the health and fitness information you already track with Apple.
