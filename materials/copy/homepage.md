# Push-Up Tracker and Training App for iPhone & Apple Watch: Pushanova 3
Track your push-ups, follow a structured training program to 100 or workout freely. A simple way to get stronger through push-ups.

[**Download on the App Store**](https://apps.apple.com/app/push-up-counter-pushanova/id6451240468)

## Start Where You Are

Whether you're doing your first push-ups or building up to 100, Pushanova gives you room to progress at your own pace. Start with manageable workouts, choose between a preset program or free mode, and take as much rest as you need -- between workouts or sets. No leaderboards, no pressure, and no penalties for slowing down. The app is there for you whether you want to do 10 quick push-ups while waiting for coffee, squeeze in a few during HIIT, or do a full push-up workout.

## How Pushanova Works

No complicated setup or lengthy registration. Put your iPhone on the floor or your Apple Watch on your wrist and start your workout. Pushanova counts your push-ups and tracks your pace while you work out on iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. Apple Watch, compatible heart rate headphones<sup>*</sup>, and Bluetooth heart-rate straps can provide heart rate and calorie data. After your workout, see a detailed summary and track your progress with analytics and workout history.

## Privacy First Workout App

Push-up tracking does not take photos or record, upload, or send video or other camera media. There is no user account and no data is ever sold to anyone. Pushanova is built around Apple's privacy technologies. Your workouts sync privately across your devices via iCloud and your health data is handled by Apple Health. 

### Ready for Your First Workout?

Start with a simple push-up workout, see how Pushanova works, and decide where you want to go from there. Core features are free to use, and you can finish your first workout in just a few minutes.
