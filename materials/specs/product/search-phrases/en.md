# English (United States)

Country: United States. Language: `en`. Month: September 2026.

Tool: DataForSEO. Google Ads search volume is the monthly average. Google organic results were checked the same month.

**Homepage winner: push up counter.** About 1,600 searches a month. Competition is low, and the top-of-page bid is about $2, so people are shopping for an app. The results page is App Store and Play Store listings plus small counter sites (Motion Coach, PUSHapp, Jimna). A product site can rank here. The homepage title carries this phrase. The H1 still promises 100 push-ups.

**Features phrase: 100 push-up program.** About 110 searches a month. Smaller than the counter phrase, and it matches the features page. The features title names the program and automatic counting so the two pages do not share one title.
