## 1. At a glance

**Pushanova** is a native push-up training app for iPhone, Apple Watch, and iPad.

It helps people get stronger at push-ups through a progressive program to 100, unrestricted Freestyle workouts, automatic counting, and a clear view of progress — without turning a simple exercise into a complicated fitness system.

### One-sentence description

Pushanova is a native Apple push-up training app that helps people progress toward 100 push-ups, train freely, automatically count workouts, and see their habit and progress on iPhone, Apple Watch, and iPad.

### Positioning

**Pushanova is a push-up training companion for Apple’s ecosystem.**

It sits between a simple repetition counter and a traditional fitness coaching app: simple enough to start immediately, sophisticated enough to support structured training, freestyle workouts, health data, and long-term progress.

### What it is

- A purpose-built push-up trainer, not a generic workout logger
- A native Apple product: iPhone, Watch, iPad, Health, widgets, Live Activities
- A habit product as much as a counting product
- Welcoming to beginners and useful to experienced users
- Compact enough to use between other training, or for a quick set in the kitchen

### What it is not

- Not a social network, leaderboard, or competition app
- Not a general gym or multi-exercise tracker
- Not a form-coaching or “AI trainer” product
- Not a streak app that punishes a missed day
- Not an account-based cloud fitness platform
- Not an ad-supported product

## 2. Vision

Help people become stronger through push-ups without turning a simple exercise into a complicated fitness system.

Pushanova is not just a push-up counter. Counting, pacing, history, analytics, Apple Watch, and widgets all serve one purpose: helping people train consistently and progress toward their goals.

The product idea is that **technology should disappear**. Someone should be able to start a workout and move, while iPhone, Apple Watch, counting, heart rate, calories, Health, and device sync work together in the background.

Pushanova is intentionally different from fitness products built around competition or intimidation.

**It does not focus on:**

- Leaderboards
- Social comparison
- Competitive rankings

- One rigid training methodology

**It aims to be:**

- Flexible
- Accessible
- Compact

- Easy to understand

**The philosophy:** encourage people to show up and get stronger at their own pace.

## 5. Product principles

### Native Apple experience

Pushanova uses Apple platforms as a foundation, not as a generic runtime. The experience follows native patterns, system technologies, accessibility, and what each device is good at.

### Simplicity

The workout stays the focus. The app avoids unnecessary configuration, social features, leaderboards, and complicated fitness concepts.

### Flexibility

People choose a Level workout or Freestyle each time they train. They can switch between them from one session to the next and keep going freely after completing a level. The app never makes them commit to one training style.

### Fits alongside other training

Pushanova is meant to live next to other exercise, not replace someone’s whole routine. A quick set between HIIT intervals, a few push-ups during a gym session, or ten reps on Apple Watch while waiting for coffee are all valid uses.

### Reliability

An in-progress workout is treated as important user data. If someone leaves the app, the phone rings, or the app is interrupted, the active workout should be restored when they return.

### Accessibility

The interface should remain understandable and usable across different abilities, devices, screen sizes, and interaction preferences.

### Global by design

Pushanova is built for worldwide use, not translated as an afterthought. Layouts, typography, numbers, and wording are designed to work across languages, including right-to-left languages, and different numeral systems.

### Privacy

No Pushanova account. Counting does not take photos or video of the user’s face. Workout data stays on the user’s devices and in their own iCloud if they use it. There is no Pushanova social graph. Data is not sold. There are no ads.

### No ads

No ads, ever.

## 6. Core experience

The app is organized around four tabs: **Training**, **Workouts**, **Calendar**, and **Progress**.

### 6.1 100 push-up training program

A progressive program of **50 levels in 3 stages** guides people toward the ability to perform 100 push-ups, at their own pace.

- **Levels 1–10** are the free foundation.
- **Levels 11–50** continue the path to 100 and are part of Premium.
- People **pick a starting level**. There is no placement test.
- A Level is one structured workout, not a permanent mode selection.
- Every level has preset sets and repetition targets. Levels 1–49 are typically five sets. The final level is **one set of 100**.
- After completing the structured path, people can keep training in Freestyle.

Following the program is optional. Freestyle is available at any time, and choosing it never prevents someone from returning to a Level later.

**How to talk about it:** a clear path to 100, at your own pace — not a 30-day challenge that fails if you miss a day.

### 6.2 Freestyle

Freestyle is unrestricted workout mode.

Users decide how many push-ups to do and how many sets to perform. The app observes and records the workout rather than forcing a prescribed routine. Sets are captured as the person trains.

Choosing Freestyle affects only that workout. Someone can do a Level workout now, choose Freestyle an hour later or the next day, and return to the program whenever they want.

**How to talk about it:** the app follows the user, not the other way around.

### 6.3 From Level to Freestyle in one workout

Completing a Level finishes the planned part of the workout; it does not end the session. Pushanova celebrates the milestone and, if the person keeps going, immediately lets the same workout continue in Freestyle.

Additional push-ups extend the final planned set. After a pause, the person can add as many unrestricted sets as they want. The workout ends only when they choose **Complete Workout**.

Think of this as **extra credit**: optional training beyond the Level target. It still counts and is saved, but it is never required to complete the Level and there is no penalty for stopping at the target. Extra credit is not a third workout mode; it is Freestyle after Level success.

**How to talk about it:** structure when you want it, freedom when you want it — even in the same workout. Level and Freestyle complement one another; neither is a permanent choice.

### 6.4 Automatic push-up tracking

Push-ups can be counted automatically so the user can focus on the exercise.

**On iPhone and iPad:** place the device on the floor and do push-ups toward the front camera. The app counts repetitions as the user moves.

**On Apple Watch:** counting uses wrist motion. That makes Watch the natural choice for push-ups on bars, benches, and other elevated surfaces, or whenever the phone should stay in a pocket.

**Tap to Count** is an optional backup: taps on the screen register repetitions.

People can also log a workout by hand, including sets and repetition counts, and correct counts after the session.

**How to talk about it:** start a workout and move. The counting method stays in the background. Do not name camera technologies or sensors in marketing.

### 6.5 Rest

Rest between sets is a suggestion, not a lock.

In a Level workout, the app recommends **90 seconds** after a planned set. It counts down, then counts up to show how long the person has actually been resting. In Freestyle, a pause with no new repetition ends the current set and starts an unrestricted count-up rest timer. The next push-up ends rest and starts the next set.

A short countdown prepares the user before the first repetition.

**How to talk about it:** the app suggests recovery; it does not punish rest or block the next set.

### 6.6 Apple Watch

Pushanova includes a **standalone Apple Watch app**. A workout can be performed entirely on Watch, including when iPhone is not nearby.

When both devices take part, they can **mirror and synchronize** workout state. iPhone can act as a companion display while Watch does the tracking.

Watch uses **haptics** to confirm repetitions and sets, so people can train with less looking at the screen.

The Watch workout includes system **Now Playing**, so people can keep listening to music or podcasts without a custom music player inside Pushanova.

**How to talk about it:** a real Watch training app, not a remote for the phone. Phone and Watch stay in sync when used together.

### 6.7 During the workout

A live session shows the information that matters while moving:

- Current phase (get ready, work, rest)
- Repetition count and set
- Timer
- Current and average pace
- Heart rate, when available
- Active calories
- Best set — the personal record for most push-ups in one set, across all workouts

The workout can continue on the **Lock Screen** and in **Dynamic Island**: phase, timer, repetitions, and set stay visible without returning to the full app.

On Watch, Always On display is supported so the session remains glanceable.

**Personality:** when someone approaches or breaks a best-set record, the workout celebrates the moment. This is a personal record, not a social score.

### 6.8 After the workout

Every completed session is saved.

On the **Workouts** tab, people can reopen a session to see sets, duration, calories, whether it was a program level or Freestyle, notes, and a feeling rating. They can share, edit set counts, or delete a workout.

If a workout felt bad, people can optionally tag what got in the way (for example breathing, pain, focus, energy, form, or environment). That is self-reported context, not automatic form scoring.

Completed workouts can be saved to **Apple Health** when the user allows it.

### 6.9 Workout sharing

Completed workouts can be turned into polished **share cards** or **post-workout photos** with stats overlaid.

Sharing is optional and personal. There is no in-app feed and no public leaderboard.

People may also submit a post-workout photo to be considered for the app’s official social channels. Valid submissions may receive a promo code for a year of Pushanova Premium. Being featured is not guaranteed.

### 6.10 Workout calendar

**Calendar** is a first-class tab for seeing which days someone trained. It makes consistency visible over weeks and months.

Calendar is a **Premium** feature.

### 6.11 Weekly Recap

Weekly Recap is this week compared with last week: push-ups, workouts, duration, energy, pace, and cadence.

It has its own screen and a **Home Screen widget**.

Weekly Recap is a **Premium** feature.

### 6.12 Workout analytics

People can review training through interactive charts inspired by Apple’s Health app: information-rich without becoming visually complicated.

Analytics can include:

- Push-up counts
- Sets
- Pace (seconds per push-up)
- Cadence (push-ups per minute)
- Repetition timing
- Workout duration
- Heart rate
- Calories / active energy
- Historical progress over recent weeks

Detailed trend charts and richer per-workout pace, cadence, and heart-rate views are **Premium**. The workout log itself is available without Premium.

### 6.13 Momentum

**Momentum** shows how consistent training has been *lately* — not how much someone has accumulated over time.

It appears as a simple gauge at the top of Progress, with lifetime totals underneath (push-ups, workouts, minutes, calories) and a **best-set record**.

- Recent training days fill the meter; older days fade.
- A long break lowers Momentum even if lifetime totals remain high.
- Missing a day does **not** reset the meter to zero.
- Extra workouts on the same day do not stack.
- The meter cares about showing up regularly, not about volume, personal records, or workout length.

Mental model: **habit temperature**, not a trophy and not a streak you break.

The same snapshot is available as a Home Screen, Lock Screen, and Apple Watch widget.

Momentum on Progress, lifetime totals, and best set are **free**.

### 6.14 Training reminders

Optional local reminders help people come back. Users choose a time of day, and either a repeating interval (every N days) or specific weekdays.

Reminders support the habit story. They are not streaks and not public accountability.

### 6.15 iPad

Pushanova is a full iPad app: training, history, progress, analytics, and Health — not an iPhone layout stretched to a larger screen.

### 6.16 Apple Health

Pushanova saves completed workouts to Apple Health when authorized, and can use health metrics such as heart rate and active energy during and after training.

The Health activity type is strength training. The in-app experience remains push-ups only.

### 6.17 Manual workout logging

If a session happened without automatic counting, users can add a push-up workout by hand, including sets and repetition counts.

## 8. Access and pricing

A portion of the app is free. Pushanova Premium unlocks the rest of the path to 100 and the richer history tools.

Purchases go through the App Store. Users can restore purchases and redeem offer codes on iPhone. Subscriptions can be canceled in Apple ID settings.

Premium is tied to the purchasing Apple ID. **Family Sharing is not enabled** for these products.

### Included for everyone

- Levels 1–10
- Freestyle
- Automatic counting, Tap to Count, and manual logging
- Workout log (list, notes, feeling, share, edit, delete)
- Momentum, lifetime totals, and best-set record
- Live Activities / Dynamic Island / Lock Screen during a workout
- Momentum widget
- Apple Watch training for free levels
- Training reminders
- Workout sharing
- Apple Health integration
- iCloud sync of the user’s own data
- No account required

### Pushanova Premium

- The complete program through level 50 (the path to 100)
- Workout calendar
- Weekly Recap (screen and widget)
- Detailed progress charts
- Richer per-workout pace, cadence, and heart-rate detail

### How Premium is sold

**How to talk about price:** an affordable alternative to expensive fitness subscriptions. Do not imply that the full path to 100 is free.

## 9. Trust, privacy, and reliability

### No account

Anyone can start without creating an account. No email, password, server login, or Sign in with Apple.

### Data

Workouts live on the user’s devices. If the user uses iCloud with the app, that same personal history can appear on their other devices. That is the user’s iCloud, not a Pushanova social product.

Counting on iPhone and iPad uses the front camera to detect movement. It does **not** take, save, or upload photos or video of the user’s face. Optional post-workout photos exist only when the user chooses to take or share one.

The app may use standard product analytics and crash reporting. Workout photos, Health data, and camera-based counting data are not used for advertising. Data is not sold.

### Reliability

An active workout is designed to survive the app being closed or crashing. The session can be restored; the work is not thrown away.

### Support

People can contact the developer directly from the app. There is no call center and no user-account dashboard. Offer codes, restore, privacy policy, and terms are available in Settings.

## 10. Brand voice

**Tone:** calm, clear, encouraging, adult. Capable without being macho. Precise without being technical.

**Personality:** a serious training tool that still has a little delight (the Momentum gauge, a best-set celebration, a share card). Delight is earned; it is not a mascot brand.

### Club Pushanova

The app’s full name is **Club Pushanova**. **Pushanova** is the everyday short form. Neither name is translated.

Club Pushanova is also a subtle imaginative setting: a lightly magical, laid-back place where doing a push-up is all it takes to belong. Membership is not bought, ranked, or tied to an account. A push-up is the membership.

The mood is warm, breezy, welcoming, and gently tropical. Tropical sounds can occasionally mark level completion, workout completion, and reminders; restrained artwork can hint at the same world. These touches should feel like small moments of hospitality and escape, never dominate the workout or turn the app into a novelty.

Keep the idea understated. “Club” is an emotional frame, not a social feature, community, or exclusive members-only system. Pushanova remains a focused, credible training tool first.

**Creative reference (internal only):** the relaxed, welcoming escapism of Wham!’s “Club Tropicana,” co-written by George Michael. Treat it as mood inspiration, not material to quote or imitate, and never imply an affiliation or endorsement.

### LGBTQ+ belonging

Club Pushanova explicitly welcomes LGBTQ+ people around the world. Every sexual orientation, gender identity, and gender expression belongs. This is a lasting brand commitment, not seasonal Pride decoration or a message used only in selected markets. The tropical club motif may stay subtle; the welcome should not be ambiguous.

Creatively, the club can carry a quiet queer warmth: joy, color, freedom, chosen belonging, and room for self-expression. Include LGBTQ+ people naturally and respectfully in copy, imagery, artwork, localization, and creator relationships. Avoid stereotypes, tokenism, gendered assumptions about strength, and treating straight or cisgender people as the default.

Keep the commitment global while protecting people. Do not erase the principle to satisfy hostile markets, but adapt individual executions when visibility could endanger users, contributors, or local partners. The app does not need to know or infer anyone’s identity for them to belong; do not collect sensitive identity data merely for marketing.

**Words to prefer**

- Train, show up, progress, habit, consistency, at your own pace
- Companion, simple, native, on Apple Watch
- Freestyle, Momentum, Weekly Recap, best set
- Welcome, belong, room for you

**Words to avoid**

- Crush, shred, no excuses, beast mode, become a machine, get jacked
- Streak (for Momentum)
- AI coach, 100% guaranteed detection
- Community, followers, ranking, leaderboard
- Challenge that you “fail”
- Gender stereotypes, tokenism, and assumptions about who strength training is for

**Club Pushanova** and **Pushanova** are not translated.

## 15. Differentiators

Lead with these. They are true and hard to copy as a bundle.

1. **Purpose-built for push-ups**, not a generic workout template
2. **Native** across iPhone, Apple Watch, and iPad
3. **Standalone Apple Watch app**, including elevated-surface and phone-away training
4. **Phone and Watch as one workout** when used together
5. **Program + Freestyle without lock-in** — choose either for every workout, or flow from a completed Level into optional extra credit in the same session
6. **Automatic counting** that stays out of the way
7. **Momentum** as habit temperature, not a streak
8. **Health-inspired analytics** without dashboard clutter
9. **Live Activities and widgets** so the workout and the habit stay nearby
10. **No account, no ads**, data on the user’s Apple devices
11. **Reliability** — an interrupted workout comes back
12. **Global by design**, including right-to-left
13. **Affordable Premium** versus expensive all-in-one fitness subscriptions
14. **Fits beside other training** — a compact tool, not a lifestyle network

## 16. Onboarding, in one line

The app introduces itself in three promises: follow a plan toward 100 push-ups; place the iPhone on the floor or start on Apple Watch and let it count; view history and progress over time. It then asks for Apple Health and, optionally, notifications.
