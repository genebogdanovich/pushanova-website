# Search phrases

Measured September 2026. The product paper wins when a louder phrase fights the product. The phrase goes in the page `<title>` and meta description. The H1 stays a sentence.

## English (United States)

Tool: DataForSEO. Google Ads search volume is the monthly average. Google organic results were checked the same month.

**Homepage winner: push up counter.** About 1,600 searches a month. Competition is low, and the top-of-page bid is about $2, so people are shopping for an app. The results page is App Store and Play Store listings plus small counter sites (Motion Coach, PUSHapp, Jimna). A product site can rank here. The homepage title carries this phrase. The H1 still promises 100 push-ups.

**Features phrase: 100 push-up program.** About 110 searches a month. Smaller than the counter phrase, and it matches the features page. The features title names the program and automatic counting so the two pages do not share one title.

### Rejected

| Phrase | Volume | Why it lost |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| push ups | 40,500 | The exercise. Bids are a few cents. |
| push up challenge | 2,400 | Results are charity events, 30-day articles, and magazines. Not an app query. January peak. |
| count push ups | 590 | Larger than “push up app,” but the bid is $0.02–$0.47. |
| push up app | 390 | Same install intent as the winner, and a smaller query. Results are mostly store listings. |
| push up counter app | 320 | Same intent, smaller. |
| push up tracker | 170 | About a tenth of “push up counter.” |
| push up program | 170 | Close to the product, small demand. The features page uses the more specific 100-push-up program phrase. |
| apple watch push ups | 10 | True, and too rare to be the title. Apple Watch stays in the sentence because it is a product fact. |
| push up counter iphone | — | Below the reporting floor. iPhone stays in the title because it is a product fact. |

`push up counter` and `pushup counter` are the same keyword.

## Russian (Runet)

Tool: Yandex Wordstat, last 30 days, all regions. Google Ads has no Russia location. Intent comes from queries that contain the phrase. The similar-phrase list was ignored.

**Homepage winner: программа отжиманий.** About 3,800 searches in 30 days. Child queries are a plan: from the floor, at home, for men, 100 push-ups. That matches the product.

**Features phrase: программа 100 отжиманий.** About 440 searches in 30 days. More specific than the homepage phrase, so the two pages do not share one title.

### Rejected

| Phrase | 30-day count | Why it lost |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| отжимания | 246,500 | The exercise, plus a school math problem that accounts for a large share. |
| 100 отжиманий | 3,900 | “Every day,” calories, and 100 squats plus 100 sit-ups. The H1 can still promise 100 push-ups. |
| счетчик отжиманий | 973 | Child queries are a metal station people buy for the ГТО test. “Счетчик отжиманий приложение” is about 26. |
| приложение для отжиманий | 237 | A real app query, and a small one. |
