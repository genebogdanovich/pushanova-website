home.qna.items.0.question|||Do I need an Apple Watch to use Pushanova?
home.qna.items.0.paragraphs.0|||No. Pushanova can track push-ups with an iPhone or iPad. Apple Watch adds wrist-based tracking and supports elevated push-ups on benches and horizontal bars.
home.qna.items.1.question|||Can I train on Apple Watch without my iPhone nearby?
home.qna.items.1.paragraphs.0|||Yes. You can start and complete a workout on Apple Watch while your iPhone is not nearby. The Watch app focuses on workouts. Workout history and analytics are available on iPhone and iPad.
home.qna.items.1.paragraphs.1|||When you use Apple Watch and iPhone together, Apple Watch can track your push-ups while iPhone displays the synchronized workout.
home.qna.items.2.question|||How does Pushanova count push-ups?
home.qna.items.2.paragraphs.0|||On iPhone and iPad, the front camera measures changes in the distance between your face and the device. On Apple Watch, Pushanova analyzes the movement of your wrist. You can also enable a setting that counts one push-up each time you tap the screen.
home.qna.items.3.question|||Can I use Pushanova without allowing camera access?
home.qna.items.3.paragraphs.0|||Yes. On iPhone, Pushanova can use another on-device sensor when camera access is unavailable. On both iPhone and iPad, you can enable counting by tapping the screen. Apple Watch does not use a camera.
home.qna.items.4.question|||Does Pushanova record or upload photos or video while counting push-ups?
home.qna.items.4.paragraphs.0|||No. Automatic counting does not take photos or record, save, or upload video. The camera only measures distance, and Pushanova does not use it to identify you.
home.qna.items.5.question|||What kinds of push-ups are supported?
home.qna.items.5.paragraphs.0|||iPhone and iPad support standard floor push-ups and push-ups from your knees. Apple Watch also supports elevated push-ups on a bench or horizontal bar.
home.qna.items.5.paragraphs.1|||Push-ups on parallel handles with your palms facing each other are not supported. Knuckle push-ups may work, but counting can be less accurate.
home.qna.items.6.question|||Will Pushanova count every push-up correctly?
home.qna.items.6.paragraphs.0|||No automatic tracking method can count every movement correctly. Device position, form, and speed can affect the result. After a workout, you can correct the number of push-ups in each existing set.
home.qna.items.7.question|||What if I cannot do one full push-up yet?
home.qna.items.7.paragraphs.0|||You can begin with the early levels, which contain small sets. Push-ups from your knees are supported. You can repeat a level and progress at your own pace.
home.qna.items.8.question|||Can experienced athletes use Pushanova with other training?
home.qna.items.8.paragraphs.0|||Yes. You can start at any level or train freely. Pushanova can record push-up sets during cardio or strength-training exercises.
home.qna.items.9.question|||What is the Pushanova 100 push-up program?
home.qna.items.9.paragraphs.0|||The program has 50 levels in three stages. Each level is a structured workout with preset sets and push-up targets. The final level is one set of 100 push-ups.
home.qna.items.9.paragraphs.1|||You can start at any level, repeat a level, or skip to another level. The first 10 levels are free. Pushanova Premium unlocks levels 11–50.
home.qna.items.10.question|||Can I complete levels and also train freely?
home.qna.items.10.paragraphs.0|||Yes. You can complete a level in one workout and train freely in another. You can also continue training freely after you complete a level in the same workout.
home.qna.items.11.question|||Can I train at my own pace and take rest days?

home.qna.items.11.paragraphs.1|||During a level workout, Pushanova suggests 90 seconds of rest between planned sets. You can continue earlier or rest longer. When you train freely, there is no fixed rest target.
home.qna.items.12.question|||Can I create a custom workout?
home.qna.items.12.paragraphs.0|||You cannot build and save a preset sequence of sets before a workout. When you train freely, you decide how many push-ups and sets to perform while you train, and Pushanova records the workout.
home.qna.items.13.question|||Does Pushanova work with Apple Health?
home.qna.items.13.paragraphs.0|||Yes. With your permission, Pushanova saves completed strength-training workouts to Apple Health, including duration, active calories, and heart rate when available. Push-up and set counts are not saved to Apple Health.
home.qna.items.13.paragraphs.1|||Apple Health permission is required for workouts on Apple Watch. It is optional on iPhone and iPad.
home.qna.items.14.question|||Does Pushanova track heart rate and calories?
home.qna.items.14.paragraphs.0|||Yes. Pushanova estimates active calories even when no heart-rate device is available. Heart rate can come from Apple Watch, compatible heart-rate earbuds, or a Bluetooth heart-rate monitor.
home.qna.items.14.paragraphs.1|||Heart-rate sensing with compatible earbuds requires iOS 26 or later on iPhone, or iPadOS 26 or later on iPad.
home.qna.items.15.question|||Is Pushanova available on iPad?

home.qna.items.16.question|||Does Pushanova work without an internet connection?
home.qna.items.16.paragraphs.0|||Yes. You can start and save a workout without an internet connection. iCloud synchronization resumes when your device is online again. App Store purchases require an internet connection.

home.qna.items.17.paragraphs.0|||No. Pushanova does not require an account or membership. You can use the app without providing an email address or creating a password. Pushanova Premium is optional and is purchased through the App Store.

The title at the top is <strong>Push-Ups</strong>.</p><ul><li>Tap <strong>Level</strong> to complete a structured workout from the program.</li><li>Tap <strong>Freestyle</strong> to train without preset sets or push-up targets.</li></ul><p>A short countdown gives you time to get into position. support.sections.0.items.1.question|||How do I choose a level?
support.sections.0.items.1.answer|||<p>On iPhone or iPad, open <strong>Training</strong> and tap <strong>Choose Level</strong>. On Apple Watch, tap <strong>Choose</strong> next to Level.</p><p>The program has 50 levels in three stages. You can start at any level available to you. Completing the required push-ups advances the program to the next level.</p><p>Levels 1–10 are free. Levels 11–50 require Pushanova Premium.</p>
support.sections.0.items.2.question|||How do I train without the program?
support.sections.0.items.2.answer|||<p>Start <strong>Freestyle</strong> on iPhone, iPad, or Apple Watch. You decide how many push-ups and sets to perform.</p><p>When Pushanova detects a rest, it finishes the current set. Your next push-up begins a new set. Tap <strong>Complete Workout</strong> when you are finished. You can also continue freely after you finish a level in the same workout.</p>
support.sections.0.items.3.question|||What do the set boxes mean?
support.sections.0.items.3.answer|||<p>On iPhone and iPad, the <strong>Sets</strong> row shows the sets in the current workout. Each box represents one set.</p><p>During a program workout:</p><ul><li>Upcoming boxes show the planned push-up targets.</li><li>The highlighted box shows the current set.</li><li>The large number shows how many push-ups remain in the current planned set.</li><li>A completed box shows how many push-ups you performed.</li><li>Additional sets appear after you continue beyond the program target.</li></ul><p>When you train freely, each box appears after Pushanova finishes a set. The large number shows the push-up count for the current set.</p><p>You can scroll the row when it contains many sets. Apple Watch shows text such as <strong>Set 2 of 5</strong> instead of boxes.</p>
support.sections.0.items.4.question|||How should I position my iPhone or iPad?
support.sections.0.items.4.answer|||<p>For automatic camera counting, place the device flat on the floor with the screen facing up. Keep the front camera under your face, use good light, and keep your face in view. Do not prop the device at an angle.</p><p>If Camera access is unavailable on iPhone, place the top edge of the phone near your face. The screen may briefly turn black when your face is close. This is expected.</p><p>If you do not use Camera access on iPad, turn on <strong>Tap to Count</strong> and tap the screen for each push-up.</p>

support.sections.1.items.1.question|||What should I do if Pushanova misses or adds push-ups?
support.sections.1.items.1.answer|||<p>Automatic counting can miss or add push-ups. Device position, lighting, movement, form, and speed can affect the result.</p><p>On iPhone or iPad:</p><ul><li>Place the device flat on the floor.</li><li>Keep your face visible to the front camera.</li><li>Use good light.</li><li>Complete a clear downward and upward movement.</li><li>Keep other movement away from the camera.</li></ul><p>On Apple Watch:</p><ul><li>Wear the band firmly and comfortably.</li><li>Complete a clear downward and upward movement.</li><li>Avoid moving the Watch arm separately from the push-up.</li></ul><p>You can use <strong>Tap to Count</strong> when automatic counting is not suitable. After the workout, you can correct the counts for completed sets.</p>
support.sections.1.items.2.question|||What should I do if automatic counting does not work at all?
support.sections.1.items.2.answer|||<ol><li>Confirm that the device is in the correct position.</li><li>On iPhone or iPad, check Camera access for Pushanova.</li><li>On Apple Watch, check Apple Health access and wear Watch firmly on your wrist.</li><li>Close and reopen Pushanova.</li><li>Start a short test workout.</li><li>If counting still does not work, use <strong>Report an Issue</strong> from the completed workout.</li></ol><p>On iPad without Camera access, use <strong>Tap to Count</strong>.</p>

support.sections.1.items.3.answer|||<p>When Camera access is unavailable, iPhone can use another built-in counting method. The screen may briefly turn black when your face is close to the top of the phone.</p><p>This is expected and does not mean that Pushanova is taking a photo or recording video.</p>
support.sections.1.items.4.question|||How do I count push-ups by tapping the screen?
support.sections.1.items.4.answer|||<p>Open Settings in Pushanova and turn on <strong>Tap to Count</strong>. During a workout, each tap on the workout screen counts one push-up.</p>
support.sections.1.items.5.question|||Can I correct or add push-ups after a workout?

To record additional work as a separate session, open <strong>Workouts</strong>, tap <strong>Add Workout</strong>, and enter the sets by hand.</p>

support.sections.3.items.2.question|||What does Pushanova save to Apple Health?
support.sections.3.items.2.answer|||<p>With your permission, Pushanova saves a completed strength-training workout and active calories to Apple Health. Duration and heart rate are included when available.</p><p>Push-up counts and set counts remain in Pushanova. Apple Health does not store those counts.</p><p>Pushanova can read supported Health information, such as heart rate, active calories, and body mass, when you allow it. This information is used for workouts and calorie estimates.</p>
support.sections.3.items.3.question|||Why is my workout missing from Apple Health?
support.sections.3.items.3.answer|||<p>Check the following:</p><ul><li>Apple Health access allows Pushanova to save workouts.</li><li>The workout contained at least one counted push-up.</li><li>You completed the workout and reached the summary.</li><li>No save error appeared on the summary.</li></ul><p>On Apple Watch, Health access must remain enabled during the workout.</p>

support.sections.3.items.6.question|||How are active calories calculated?
support.sections.3.items.6.answer|||<p>Pushanova uses active calories from Apple Health when they are available. The workout and basic summary are still saved without Premium.</p>
